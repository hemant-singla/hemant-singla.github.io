Independent public-data studyWalmartRetail · Demand planning

Demand forecasting & review priorities

Completed

Tested three forecasting methods on five years of daily Walmart sales (200 products across two stores), keeping the final four weeks hidden until every choice was locked. Then tested which rule should pick the five items a planner reviews each week.

Results: the best model was 21% more accurate than the standard week-over-week baseline; its 90% forecast ranges caught 89% of actual sales; and a 5-item weekly review list caught 5× more forecast error than picking at random.
