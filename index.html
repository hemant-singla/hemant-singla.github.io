Independent public-data studyWalmartRetail · Demand planning

Demand forecasting & review priorities

Completed

Compared seasonal-naive, ETS and LightGBM forecasts on 200 Walmart product-store series with a 60-origin weekly rolling backtest and a sealed 28-day holdout, then tested which rule should pick the five series a planner reviews each week.

Results: LightGBM cut holdout forecast error 21% vs. the seasonal baseline (RMSSE 0.733 vs 0.932); calibrated 90% ranges covered 89% of actuals; a 5-item review list caught 5× more forecast error than random picks.
