Apple · Technology / electronics

Supplier geography and concentration.

Where is the disclosed supplier footprint concentrated, and which suppliers warrant continuity due diligence?

Independent public-data study · Planned.

Company context

Apple reported reduced capacity at its primary iPhone 14 Pro assembly facility in Zhengzhou, leading to lower expected shipments and longer customer waits. Apple said it was working with its supplier to restore production; this study examines the disclosed footprint, not the effect of that recovery effort.

Data and scope

Documented 2022 disruption; FY2023 supplier footprint published in 2024. Additional comparable editions only after coverage audit.

Proposed analysis

SQL supplier/location bridge with normalized countries/regions. Geographic footprint concentration, single-region supplier flags, mapping and hypothetical region-disruption exposure. Prefer transparent rules over unsupported predictive ML.

How I will test the geographic flags

Audit supplier aliases and country/region normalization against the source PDF. Deduplicate supplier-location pairs and distinguish geographic disclosures from exact facility addresses. Test concentration flags under alternative aggregation rules; do not weight them by undisclosed spend or capacity.

The sourcing handoff

A supplier-footprint map and a prioritized continuity-question list: which locations require further capacity, substitutability or business-continuity evidence? Geographic exposure prompts due diligence, not an automatic supplier replacement.

Planned deliverables

Supplier footprint map, concentration dashboard, prioritized continuity due-diligence list and risk memo.

Status & limitations

Extraction and analysis have not started. Disclosed locations describe the supplier footprint, not spend shares or production capacity. Locations may be regions rather than individual facilities; component substitutability is unknown. The study cannot establish output losses or optimal replacements.
